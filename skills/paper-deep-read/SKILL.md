# Paper deep read

1. Locate the paper (`find_papers` by title/author, or `search_library`).
2. Call `read_paper` 2-3 times with focused questions: (a) problem and motivation, (b) method details / key idea, (c) experiments, results and limitations.
3. Explain in the user's language with sections: 背景/Motivation, 方法/Method, 实验/Results, 局限/Limitations, 一句话总结. Mention page numbers when the evidence has them, e.g. (p. 5) [n].
